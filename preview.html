Articles: A, An, The

Lesson 4B — 25 questions

Gap Fill

I saw ___ dog running down the street.

Answer: a

a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound — listen to the sound, not the letter.

She works as ___ engineer at a software company.

Answer: an

a/an + job: she's a doctor, he's an artist.

My sister is ___ university student in Kyiv.

Answer: a

Say the word aloud — university, uniform, European, one — all start with a consonant sound despite their vowel letter.

We arrived ___ hour early for the flight.

Answer: an

hour, honest, honour — the 'h' is silent, so use 'an'.

Usain Bolt is considered ___ fastest runner in history.

Answer: the

Superlative = the + adjective + -est (or the most + adjective).

Thousands of tourists visit ___ River Thames every year.

Answer: the

the + River [Name] / the [Name] Ocean / the [Name] Sea.

Error Spot

She's a artist and paints amazing portraits.

Correction: an

'Artist' begins with a vowel sound — use 'an': 'She's an artist.'

He's an teacher at a secondary school.

Correction: a

'Teacher' begins with a consonant sound — use 'a': 'He's a teacher.'

I looked up and saw a Sun shining brightly.

Correction: the

'The Sun' is unique — there's only one, so it always takes 'the'.

That was a best film I've seen this year.

Correction: the

Superlative adjectives always take 'the': 'That was the best film...'

We took a boat trip along a River Danube.

Correction: the

River names always take 'the', not 'a': 'along the River Danube.'

He was a only survivor of the crash.

Correction: the

'Only' is a defining expression and always takes 'the': 'He was the only survivor.'

Multiple Choice

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Elephants are bigger than lions.

Elephants are big. Dogs are loyal. Cats sleep a lot. — general truths = no article + plural noun.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: I live in Paris.

I live in Paris / I'm from Japan — no article with most place names.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: She has lived in the United States for ten years.

Most countries: no article (France, Japan). Countries with extra words: the United Kingdom, the United States.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: He plays football every weekend.

I like tennis. He plays football. — no article with sports.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: I was at work when you called.

at work, on foot, for lunch, on Monday — learn these as fixed phrases, no article.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: I bought six bottles of water.

I bought six bottles of water. (new info) vs I drank the water you bought. (already known)

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: The meeting is on the fifth of June.

Spoken: the fifth of June. Written: 5 June / June 5th (no 'the' needed in writing).

Word Transform

I need ___ (a/an) umbrella — it's raining.

Answer: an

a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound: an umbrella, an hour, an idea.

She works as ___ (a/an) doctor in Kyiv.

Answer: a

a + consonant sound: a doctor, a nurse, a teacher.

Everest is ___ (a/an/the) highest mountain on Earth.

Answer: the

the + superlative: the highest, the best, the most beautiful.

They live near ___ (a/an/the) River Thames.

Answer: the

the + River [Name] — always.

This is ___ (a/an/the) same phone I bought last year — I just repaired it.

Answer: the

the same, the only, the first, the next — defining expressions always take 'the'.

By ___ (a/an/the) end of the week, we'd finished the whole project.

Answer: the

by the end, in the end, at the end — learn these as fixed 'the' phrases.
