Articles: a, an, the

Lesson 4B — 25 questions

Gap Fill

I saw ___ mouse in the kitchen.

Answer: a

First mention, unknown which one → a/an. Already known or mentioned → the.

She's ___ engineer and lives in Kyiv.

Answer: an

It's the SOUND that matters, not the letter: an engineer, an hour, but a university.

I bought a laptop yesterday. ___ laptop stopped working today.

Answer: the

First mention = a/an. Every mention after that = the.

That's ___ best restaurant in town.

Answer: the

Superlative → the: the best, the tallest, the most expensive.

They sailed along ___ River Danube last summer.

Answer: the

the + river/sea/ocean name — no exceptions.

You're ___ only person who understands me.

Answer: the

the only / the first / the last / the next — always 'the'.

Error Spot

She's a engineer and works in Berlin.

Correction: an

Use 'an' before a vowel sound: 'an engineer'.

Yesterday I saw the mouse in the kitchen.

Correction: a

First mention of something unknown to the listener → use 'a': 'I saw a mouse.'

Yuri Gagarin was a first man in space.

Correction: the

Defining expressions like 'the first' always use 'the': 'the first man in space'.

I want to be the doctor when I grow up.

Correction: a

Talking about a profession in general → 'a doctor', not 'the doctor'.

They sailed down a River Danube for two weeks.

Correction: the

River names always use 'the': 'the River Danube'.

This is a most beautiful painting I've ever seen.

Correction: the

Superlatives always take 'the': 'the most beautiful painting'.

Multiple Choice

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: He's a doctor.

Job = a/an + job title: a doctor, an artist, a teacher.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Dogs make loyal pets.

General statement about a whole group → plural noun, no article: Dogs are loyal.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: She grew up in Poland.

Country name, one word → no article: in Poland, in France, in Japan.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: They crossed the Atlantic Ocean by boat.

Ocean/sea name → always 'the': the Atlantic Ocean, the Black Sea.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: My cousin moved to the United Kingdom last year.

Country name with an extra word (Kingdom, States, Republic) → the. Simple one-word name → no article.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: He plays football every weekend.

Sport → no article: play football, not play the football.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: The meeting is on the fifth of June.

Spoken date → the + ordinal + of + month: the fifth of June.

Word Transform

I'd like ___ (a/an) apple, please.

Answer: an

an + vowel SOUND: an apple, an hour. a + consonant SOUND: a university, a European.

He works as ___ (a/an) teacher in a local primary school.

Answer: a

a/an + job: a teacher, an actor, a nurse.

This is ___ (a/the) best pizza I've ever eaten.

Answer: the

Superlatives always take 'the': the best, the tallest, the most expensive.

We're planning to sail across ___ (a/the) Pacific Ocean next year.

Answer: the

the + sea/ocean/river name — always.

My brother has lived in ___ (a/the) United States for ten years.

Answer: the

One-word country name → no article (Poland, France). Country name with an extra word → the (the United States).

Could you pass me ___ (a/the) salt? It's right next to you.

Answer: the

If both speaker and listener know exactly which one you mean, use 'the'.
